Gopnath Mahadev at Moti Gop Hill Station

જામજોધપુર તાલુકામાં મોટાગોપ પાસે ગોપ ડુંગર પર આવેલું ગોપનાથ મહાદેવ મંદિર - અદભુત પર્વતીય વાતાવરણ અને પવિત્ર શિખર.

Situated at the summit of Gop Dungar (Gop Hill) near Zinavari and Moti Gop in Jamnagar district, Gopnath Mahadev Temple offers breathtaking views of the cliffside precipice, surrounding wind turbines, and rolling green valleys.

Devotees can reach the hilltop shrine by climbing 600 stone steps or taking a vehicle along the winding mountain road. At the base of the hill stands the ancient 6th-century Maitraka period Gop Temple.

History & Antiquity

Temple History & Sacred Origin

Puranic Mythological Legend

Legend has it that the Gopis installed this Shivalinga on Lord Krishna's advice after the Rasalila, earning the deity the name Gopnath ("Lord of the Gopis").

6th Century CE · Maitraka Period

At the foot of Gop Hill lies the 6th-century Gop Temple (Zinavari), one of the oldest stone structures in Gujarat with pyramidal masonry architecture.

Present Day Pilgrimage

A revered shrine rated 4.7★ on Google Maps. Best visited during monsoon and Shravan month for lush green views and peaceful darshan.
